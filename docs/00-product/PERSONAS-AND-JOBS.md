# Personas and Jobs-to-be-Done

## ICT Administrator
"When something significant happens, I need one place to understand it, approve actions and prove what happened."

## Network Engineer
"I need topology, interface health, paths, flows and config changes without manually correlating several tools."

## Security Analyst
"I need endpoint + network evidence correlated around a user/device/incident."

## Technician
"I need safe diagnostics and clear escalation, not unrestricted administrator access."

## Management
"I need availability, risk, trends and major incidents without raw operational noise."

## Auditor
"I need immutable, searchable history of identity, actions, approvals and changes."
